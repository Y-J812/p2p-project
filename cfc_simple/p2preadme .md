## 📌 About "Prompt 2 Product"

**Prompt 2 Product** is an exciting hackathon / build workshop focused on turning creative AI prompts into fully functional, real-world products and software prototypes.

### Key Highlights

* **AI-Driven Innovation:** Leverage generative AI, LLMs, and prompt engineering to build faster and smarter.

* **Community & Collaboration:** Supported by **MLH** and **CSI AITR**, offering mentorship, community support, and industry standard practices.

* **Rapid Prototyping:** Turn ideas into tangible software solutions in record time.
