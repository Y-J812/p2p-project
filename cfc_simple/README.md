# prompt2product-project

Health Earth Coach
Overview
Health Earth Coach is a simple, user-friendly carbon footprint and sustainability coaching application. It helps users understand their environmental impact, track changes over time, explore what-if scenarios, and receive practical recommendations for reducing their carbon footprint.
The product combines a dashboard, carbon-footprint calculator, history and progress tracking, recommendations, scenario simulation, and an Earth-focused AI assistant.

Main Features
- Personal carbon footprint calculation
- Transport, electricity, food, waste, and lifestyle impact tracking
- Dynamic dashboard and section navigation
- History of previous footprint assessments
- Week/month progress comparison
- Personalized recommendations
- What-if carbon reduction scenarios
- AI Earth Assistant for carbon and sustainability questions
- AI progress comparison with compliments and constructive complaints
- Out-of-scope protection for unrelated questions
- Simple backend with persistent local data
- Windows .bat files for easy local startup
- Docker-ready structure for deployment

AI Assistant
The Earth Assistant is designed specifically for carbon footprint, climate, sustainability, emissions, and environmental-impact questions.
It can answer questions about:
- Carbon footprints and CO₂/CO₂e
- Climate change and global warming
- Transport and travel
- Electricity and household energy
- Food and diets
- Waste and recycling
- Shopping and lifestyle
- Water footprint
- Trees, forests, and carbon removal
- Carbon offsets
- Personal footprint analysis
- Historical footprint comparisons
- Recommendations and reduction plans
For unrelated questions, the assistant should politely explain that the question is outside the Health Earth Coach scope instead of pretending to be a general-purpose assistant.

Example:
Sorry, I can't help with that. 🌍 I'm the Health Earth Coach, so I focus on carbon footprints, climate, sustainability, emissions, and your environmental progress.

Purpose
The goal of Health Earth Coach is to make environmental impact easier to understand and easier to improve. The application is designed to turn footprint data into practical actions and encourage users through measurable progress.
